Eco-Smart Station (RainErgy)

Development and Implementation of Eco-Smart Station: Rainwater Harvesting and Hybrid Energy Solutions for Water and Charging Accessibility. University of Rizal System, College of Engineering, Antipolo City, March 2027.

100%Water filtration accuracy (10 trials)
4.64 / 4.72Expert and end-user grand means, both "Very Much Acceptable"
146Evaluators: 10 experts and 136 end-users

What it is

An integrated prototype that harvests rainwater into separate storage drums and filters it through a multi-stage unit. It generates hybrid solar and microturbine power, offers coin-operated water dispensing and device charging, and provides no-cost emergency water access. An ESP32 monitors the water level and performs automated chlorine dosing.

Method and results

Developmental Research Design guided by the ADDIE model, with iterative build, test, and refine phases. Results come from 10 trials in a controlled setting.

  • Energy generation consistency: 80%.
  • Coin interface, water dispensing, device charging, chlorine dosing, water-level monitoring, and emergency water access: 90% each.

Limits and next steps

The researchers note a controlled setting and a small expert sample. Planned future work includes laboratory water-quality testing, outdoor deployment, and cashless payment. No claim is made that the treated water is safe to drink until those tests are done.

Team

Five co-authors: John Marlon Amador Aungon, Althea Lizette Rodriguez Palustre, Daron Miguel Sigua, Shan Wesley Bachain, and Vincent Bryant Lovedioro Yuson. For details on individual contributions, please contact Shan or ask the AI.
